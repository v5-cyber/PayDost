Building the financial backbone for India's builders.

PayVlt was founded with a single mission: to ensure that Indian contractors and MSMEs never have to beg for their own hard-earned money.

The Problem We're Solving

In the Indian construction, HVAC, and civil engineering sectors, delayed payments are considered a "normal" part of doing business. Contractors often finish massive projects, only to wait 90, 120, or even 180 days to get paid. The lack of formal digital agreements, the reliance on informal WhatsApp chats, and the hesitation to sound "too aggressive" means contractors' working capital gets locked up, stifling their growth.

We realized that contractors don't just need an invoicing tool — they need a system that enforces discipline, leverages legal frameworks like the MSMED Act, and automates follow-ups without ruining client relationships.

Our Solution

PayVlt is more than just a SaaS product; it's a Financial OS built explicitly for the Indian market. We combine automated, multi-lingual WhatsApp reminders with robust digital agreements and integrated payment gateways. By enforcing the 45-day MSME payment rules, PayVlt shifts the power dynamic back to the contractor.

Our Roots in Indore

Built proudly in Indore, Madhya Pradesh — the heart of India. We understand the pulse of the Indian MSME ecosystem because we are part of it. Our team consists of engineers, legal experts, and former contractors who experienced the pain of unpaid invoices firsthand.
